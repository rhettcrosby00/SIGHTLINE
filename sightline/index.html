About Sightline

A pre-game warm-up for your eyes and mind, in four parts:

  1. RegulateBreathe to calm down and focus.
  2. ActivateWake up your eyes: tracking, jumps, and gaze holds.
  3. ChallengeFast reaction and brain drills.
  4. RehearsePicture your best play, then lock in.

Every session is shuffled so it feels new. To keep it fresh, this phone remembers which questions, scripts and problems you saw in your last few sessions and skips them for a while. No results or personal info are kept.
