Privacy

Effective October 19, 2026

The short version: Sightrep keeps your setup on your phone, with a copy saved under your key so it comes back on a new phone or in the home-screen app. We don’t sell data, show ads, or track you. The only counting is anonymous totals of first-time setup steps.

On your phone

Your settings, drill levels, My Routine, training days, your safety-waiver acceptance (its version and the date and time), your license key, and a random ID for this phone. They stay on your phone. Clearing Safari’s or Chrome’s data for the site removes them (you’ll enter your key again).

On our server

  • Leaderboard: the name you enter, your session scores, the kind of session and the date. Anyone using the leaderboard can see names and scores.
  • License keys: a one-way scramble (hash) of your key, never the key itself, plus your phone’s random ID and when the key was activated or moved. It’s how one key stays on one phone at a time.
  • Your setup, saved with your key: your settings, drill levels, My Routine, training days, last runs, leaderboard name, MOVE cone setup, Focus Check results, and your safety-waiver acceptance (its version and the date). It’s stored under the scrambled key, so when you enter your key in the home-screen app or on a new phone, your setup comes back and you don’t redo it.
  • Reviews on this site: the first name, sport or position, stars and words you send, whether you got Sightrep free or know the team, and the date. No email or account. A review is shown publicly, here, only after we check it.
  • Totals of first-time setup steps: when a new phone reaches a setup step (like accepting the waiver or adding Sightrep to the home screen), the server adds 1 to that day’s total for the step. Nothing else is kept with it: no ID, cookie, name or network address.
  • Limits against abuse: your network (IP) address, kept briefly to limit repeated requests, then deleted automatically.

Payments

Gumroad sells Sightrep for us and handles your payment and sales tax. Your card details never reach us. As the seller, we receive your email address and purchase details from Gumroad, and use them only for your key and for support. See Gumroad’s privacy policy.

Eye Check (beta)

Eye Check is optional and off until you turn it on. It uses your front camera, and the video is processed on your phone. It never leaves your phone and is never recorded.

Who helps run Sightrep

Cloudflare hosts the app and this site, Neon stores the leaderboard and key records, Gumroad handles purchases and receipts, and Google Fonts provides the typefaces.

Ages

Sightrep is for athletes 16 and older, or younger with permission from a parent, guardian or coach.

Your choices

Email support@sightrep.app to remove your leaderboard name and scores, your review, your key record, or the setup saved with your key, or with any question about your data.

Changes

If this page changes, the date at the top changes with it.
