Put Sightrep on your home screen

Sightrep installs straight from your browser. No app store needed. Then it opens like a regular app: full screen, with its own icon, and drills work on the field with no signal.

Open Sightrep

Right after you enter your key, Sightrep shows these steps for your phone. You can find them again any time in Settings → Install on home screen.

iPhone and iPad (Safari)

  1. Open app.sightrep.app in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button: the square with an arrow pointing up. Don’t see it? Tap ⋯ first.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. If you see Open as Web App, leave it on. Tap Add.
  5. Open Sightrep from your home screen and tap Paste your key. Tap Copy your key on the steps screen in Safari first (or copy it from your Gumroad page). Moving your key into the home-screen app is free, and your settings come back with it.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open app.sightrep.app in Chrome.
  2. Tap Install Sightrep on the steps screen. No button? Tap ⋮ at the top right, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Tap Install. Open Sightrep from your home screen or app list. Your key comes with it.

Samsung Internet: tap ≡, then Add page to → Home screen.

Computer (Chrome or Edge)

  1. Click Install app on the steps screen, or the install icon at the right end of the address bar. In Edge: ⋯ → Apps → Install this site as an app.
  2. Or just bookmark the page. Safari on a Mac: File → Add to Dock.

Trouble?

I don’t see “Add to Home Screen.”
On iPhone, open the page in Safari: apps like Instagram, TikTok or Gmail can’t add it. In Safari, tap ⋯ if you don’t see Share. On Android, open it in Chrome.
The app asks for my key again.
On iPhone, the home-screen app keeps its own storage, separate from Safari, so it asks once. Copy your key (from the steps screen in Safari, or your Gumroad page), then tap Paste your key. Moving it from Safari into the app is free, and your settings, levels and waiver come back with it.
Safari says Sightrep is on my home screen now.
That’s expected: your key moved into the home-screen app. Open Sightrep from its icon.
I deleted the icon.
Add it again with the same steps and enter your key. Your settings come back, because they’re saved with your key. Re-adding it counts as one of your 3 moves in 30 days.
Does it work with no signal?
Yes, once you’ve opened it with a connection. Drills run anywhere after that. It needs a connection at least once every 30 days to check your key.
